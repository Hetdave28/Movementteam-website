Who We Are

Physiotherapy that treats the cause, not just the pain.

Most people arrive at Movement Team after months of temporary relief — a painkiller here, a hot pack there, the same ache returning every week. We work differently. Every patient begins with a full biomechanical assessment: how you move, where the load is going wrong, and which structure is actually failing.

From that assessment we build a treatment plan combining manual therapy, targeted electrotherapy, and progressive exercise loading — then we teach you to keep the result. Our clinics in Maninagar and Naranpura are equipped for everything from acute sports injuries to long-term neurological rehabilitation.

Common questions

Do I need a doctor's referral to book physiotherapy?

No. You can book a physiotherapy assessment at either branch directly. If we find that imaging or an orthopaedic opinion is needed, we will arrange that for you.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends entirely on the condition. Acute muscular problems often resolve in 4–6 sessions; post-operative and neurological rehabilitation runs longer. After your first assessment you will be given an honest estimate — not an open-ended package.

Which branch should I visit?

Both branches offer the same core physiotherapy services. Choose whichever is closer — Maninagar (opposite Satyam Tower, near Maninagar Railway Station) or Naranpura (Ankur Road, near Kalupur Bank).

Do you offer home visit physiotherapy?

Yes. Home-based sessions and trained attendants are available for post-surgical, bed-ridden, elderly and stroke patients across Ahmedabad. Call the clinic to check availability in your area.

Is physiotherapy painful?

Treatment should never be unbearable. Some techniques — deep tissue release, dry needling, mobilising a stiff joint — cause short-term discomfort, but your physiotherapist will work within a level you agree to and will stop if it becomes too much.
